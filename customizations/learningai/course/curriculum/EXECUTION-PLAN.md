## Assessment and release limits

Expected evidence: ordered request/return path; distinction between transport, service and inference; correction justified by the current organizer update versus an older notice; transfer notice constrained to supplied facts; a self-review naming a reasoned revision. Rubrics support learner/educator review. No semantic grading, mastery claim or invented student results. Planning estimate: 12 minutes, not learner-tested.

Concept sources: existing Google ML and LLM references rechecked during this increment; the fictional schedule is original teaching material. Human educator, assistive-technology and real learner evaluation remain release work. No new video or full custom UI completion is claimed.
